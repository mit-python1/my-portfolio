A bit more about me

I'm passionate about bringing creativity and innovation into medical device design. With a background in biomedical engineering, I love exploring how technology can make healthcare smarter, more effective, and more accessible.

Over time I've also developed a strong interest in software engineering. For me, the exciting part is finding ways to combine both worlds using engineering skills to design devices, and software to power them with data, intelligence, and usability.

This space is where I share the projects I've worked on, the skills I've built, and the ideas I'm chasing. Thanks for stopping by. I hope you enjoy exploring my work!
