Research

Optical Imaging Lab

In the Optical Imaging Lab, I'm working on combining light rays with sound waves using a photoacoustic probe to explore new ways of detecting gum infections.

It's exciting research because it brings together two powerful tools, optics and acoustics, to capture clearer images of oral tissue. My role involves designing and testing parts of the setup so that light and sound align precisely, a step that's key to making this technology reliable for real-world medical use.
